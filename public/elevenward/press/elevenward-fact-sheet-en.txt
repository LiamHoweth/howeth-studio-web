Genre: Global football career and life RPG
Platforms: Available on iPhone and iPad; Android in development

Players: Single-player
Audience: Ages 13+
Launch languages: English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, and French

Elevenward follows one original player from prospect to retirement. Each matchweek asks the player to choose a training focus, manage risk in a decisive football moment, and live with transparent consequences across form, fitness, trust, money, relationships, contracts, transfers, trophies, and legacy.

The complete regular career is free and playable offline without an account. Optional account features require internet. There are no ads, subscriptions, loot boxes, premium currency, or energy timers. Optional permanent passes add development and positive-income boosts, extra career slots, and cosmetics. Version 1.1 pass benefits: VIP gives 1.5× weekly development and positive income, five career slots, and premium cosmetics. The focused development and income passes give 2× and stack with VIP to 3×. All-Access includes all three benefits. Current prices and Restore purchases are available in More → Shop.

Upcoming version 1.1 scope (being prepared for App Review; not yet available):
- 520 fictional clubs across 52 leagues in 48 countries, plus 48 national teams
- Promotion and relegation, domestic cups, and international competition
- Four position families and 12 archetypes
- Choice-driven weekly training and safe, balanced, or bold spotlight decisions; matches are simulated
- Career ambitions, recurring mentor stories, match and decision journals, and Hall of Fame archives
- Complete offline regular careers; optional permanent passes and online account features
